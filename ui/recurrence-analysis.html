Engineering Intervention Framework

🏗️

Full Subgrade Reconstruction

For critical zones (≥4 detections). Replace underlying sub-base with soil stabilization, geogrid reinforcement, and overlay concrete pavers or polymer asphalt.

🌧️

Drainage & Sub-drain Retrofits

For moderate zones (2-3 detections). Water logged sub-bases are the primary driver of failure. Implement trench edge drains and slot drainage channels.
